Rui Min

Hi, I am a final-year Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), co-supervised by Prof. Minhao Cheng and Prof. Yi R. (May) Fung. My research interests broadly span (Trustworthy) Machine Learning and LLM Agents.

I am currently an intern at UniPat AI. Previously, I interned at Sea AI Lab, where I worked with Dr. Tianyu Pang and Dr. Chao Du, and at Alibaba Tongyi Lab, where I focused on deep research. I received my B.Eng. from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) and Queen Mary University of London (QMUL).
